> **Summary.** Which AI app is best for writing anime fanfiction depends on how the writer works: NovelAI and Sudowrite suit people who draft prose, Character.AI and SillyTavern suit people who chat with characters, ChatGPT and Claude help with planning and editing, and Lunafic suits writers who want to play a cast through chapters in a world they build. No single app does all of this, and Character.AI has been in a Chapter 11 sale since October 12, 2026, which makes it a risky home for long work.

> Written by the Lunafic team. [Lunafic](https://lunafic.ai) is our AI roleplay app, and this guide covers it alongside other tools. Facts last checked October 25, 2026.

## Overview

Characters in anime fanfiction need to sound like the show, with their honorifics, nicknames and the way they end a sentence. Canon has to be respected until the writer breaks it on purpose. A divergence set up in chapter 2 has to still be true in chapter 12. And at the end, the writer needs clean chapters to post on sites like AO3 or Wattpad.

The apps in this guide fall into four kinds:

- **Prose co-writers** (NovelAI, Sudowrite). The writer drafts, and the AI continues or expands the text.
- **Character chat apps** (Character.AI, SillyTavern). The writer talks with one or more characters.
- **General assistants** (ChatGPT, Claude). Good at ideas and editing, not built for fiction.
- **Roleplay story apps** (Lunafic). The writer plays a character or directs, the AI plays the cast, and the story grows in chapters.

## Evaluation dimensions

Each app was checked against six questions.

1. **Canon storage.** Where do series facts and character sheets live, and when does the AI read them?
2. **Character voice.** Does a character keep their speech habits over a long story?
3. **Control of your character.** Can the writer keep an original character (OC) to themselves, or direct the whole cast?
4. **Long-story memory.** Does a fact from an early chapter survive?
5. **Output.** How does a chapter get out of the app and onto a fanfic site?
6. **Cost and setup.** What does it take to start, and what does a long story cost?

These are checks against published features and prices, not a lab benchmark.

## App profiles

### NovelAI

NovelAI is a writing tool where the writer stays in charge of every line. Paid tiers (Tablet $10, Scroll $15, Opus $25) include unlimited text generation, and Opus has the largest context and Xialong, a creative-writing model. Canon lives in the Lorebook: an entry is added to the context when one of its keys appears in recent text, or always if the writer sets it so. The trade-off is effort. Every character and rule is written by hand, and a scene with four speakers needs steering line by line. The free trial is 50 text generations.

### Sudowrite

Sudowrite is built for planning and drafting a manuscript. Its Story Bible holds the synopsis, characters, worldbuilding and outline, and its tools read it before writing. Write reads up to 20,000 words of earlier text, Chapter Continuity links up to 25 chapters, and Draft writes chapter prose from planned scenes. Sudowrite says its fiction model, Muse, does not refuse dark scenes. Plans are $10, $22 and $44 a month billed yearly, or $19, $29 and $59 month to month. It is a tool for one author: no character talks back, and anime speech habits appear only when the writer teaches them.

### Character.AI

Character.AI has fan-made characters from almost every series, and a chat starts in seconds. For fanfiction it is one chat with one character rather than chapters, and fan characters can disappear: in September 2025 it removed Disney characters after a cease-and-desist letter. Users under 18 lost open-ended chats on November 25, 2025. On October 12, 2026 the company filed for Chapter 11 to run a court-supervised sale, with Disney as the first bidder at $20 million for a non-exclusive license to its intellectual property. The app keeps running during the sale, which is expected to close by late November or early December, and it may shut down if no buyer keeps it. Writers with chats they care about can request an account data export in settings.

### SillyTavern

SillyTavern is free and open source under AGPL-3.0. It runs on any machine with Node.js 20 or later, or in Docker, and connects to many model providers or a local model. Canon goes into World Info (lorebooks), documents can be attached for the AI to reference, and group chats let several characters share a scene. The cost is setup time and the model bill. The result is a chat log, so turning it into a posted chapter is still the writer's work.

### ChatGPT and Claude

Both are useful next to another tool: finding a divergence point, checking pacing, or tightening a fight scene. Both offer Projects for canon notes and earlier chapters, and ChatGPT Free allows 5 files per project. The weak spot for fiction is voice. A cold character tends to drift toward a polite assistant tone, and confident mistakes about canon are easy to miss. OpenAI paused its planned adult mode indefinitely in March 2026.

### Lunafic

Lunafic is our app, built for long stories with a cast. For fanfiction, a writer [starts a Private world](https://lunafic.ai/docs/start-a-private-world), which only they can open, and builds the cast in the [Wiki](https://lunafic.ai/docs/the-wiki). Build with AI has a Fanfiction mode for characters from an existing series. Each character has a Series field, a Speech Style panel, Personality in three moods, Speech Examples (the docs ask for at least five lines, taken from the source when there is one), Nicknames written with the caller in brackets, and a Knowledge panel for what the character knows and does not know.

The writer picks a seat. In [MC Mode](https://lunafic.ai/features/mc-mode/) they play their OC and the AI plays everyone else, and it never writes the OC's dialogue, thoughts or actions. In [Author Mode](https://lunafic.ai/docs/two-ways-to-direct) they direct and the AI writes every character, and a passage of about 120 words or more that the writer types stays exactly as written.

For anime voices, [Narrative settings](https://lunafic.ai/docs/narrative-settings) can set the characters to speak Japanese while the story stays in English, with romaji and a translation under each line. [Story memory](https://lunafic.ai/docs/story-memory) reads the newest scenes word for word, recaps of older chapters, promises and rules, the Wiki, and the writer's notes, including secrets with the faces of who knows them. Nicknames characters start using are saved for that story. Type `/newchapter` to start a chapter. Export takes the current chapter as DOCX or HTML, or copies it with formatting, on every plan. Chats from other apps can be [imported](https://lunafic.ai/docs/import-your-chats) as a pasted log or a SillyTavern .jsonl file.

Honest limits:

- **Roleplay first.** The story grows reply by reply. Writers who want full chapter drafts from an outline may be better served by Sudowrite or NovelAI.
- **Young library.** There are fewer worlds to browse than on large chat apps, and most are anime-style. For fanfiction, the writer builds their own world.
- **Free plan limits.** 20,000 AI usage a month, the Flash model only, and up to 50 Wiki entries. Pro is on paid plans, with a 5-hour and a daily limit. When usage runs out, writing pauses until the month resets or the writer upgrades.
- **No branching inside a story for now.** For a what-if, a new session in the same world starts a separate story.
- **Paid tools.** Export works one chapter at a time. Search, find and replace, and the Memory dialog are on paid plans.

## Selection guide

| If the writer... | Consider | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Writes the prose and wants the AI to continue it | NovelAI | Line-by-line control, Lorebook for canon |
| Outlines first and wants chapter drafts | Sudowrite | Story Bible, Draft, Chapter Continuity |
| Wants a quick chat with a fan-made character | Character.AI | Largest ready-made library, but in a sale |
| Enjoys building and tuning a setup | SillyTavern | Any model, World Info, group chats |
| Needs ideas and edits next to another tool | ChatGPT or Claude | Strong at brainstorming and revision |
| Wants a cast in their own voices, chapters, and their OC left to them | Lunafic | Wiki, MC Mode, story memory, young library |

A simple test works on any app. Write the scene hardest to keep in character, set up one fact in chapter 1, and ask about it sideways a few chapters later.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Character.AI still a safe place for a long fanfic?

It still runs as of October 25, 2026, but its future depends on the Chapter 11 sale it started on October 12, 2026. If no buyer keeps the service, it may be shut down, so writers should keep their own copies of anything they care about.

### Can an AI keep Japanese honorifics and speech habits?

Most apps can when the character sheet asks for them. Lunafic sets the character language in the story settings, so Japanese dialogue can come with romaji and an English translation under each line.

### Can a fanfic chat from another app continue on Lunafic?

Yes. Move your chats takes a pasted log or a SillyTavern .jsonl file, asks who is who, and continues it as a story.

### Which of these apps can be tried for free?

All of them have a free way in: NovelAI's 50-generation trial, Sudowrite's trial, Character.AI's free tier, SillyTavern itself (models may cost money), the free tiers of ChatGPT and Claude, and Lunafic's Free plan.
