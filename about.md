This site is a small library of reference guides about AI roleplay and interactive stories.

It is written by the team behind [Lunafic](https://lunafic.ai), an AI roleplay app where you play one character and the AI plays everyone else. Because we build one of the apps in this space, we disclose it on every page.

## How we write

- The answer comes first, in plain words.
- Facts about other apps are checked on the date shown on each guide, and we update guides when things change.
- We describe other apps by what they do well, and we list Lunafic's own limits too.
- Examples from Lunafic come from real, original worlds on the site, not made-up chat logs.

## Corrections

If something is wrong or out of date, [open an issue](https://github.com/lunafic-ai/roleplay/issues) and we will fix it.
